# Examples

## Oversized software idea

Input:

```text
/side-hustle assisted prepare An AI platform that analyzes every supplement brand on Instagram and predicts sales
```

Likely direction:

- identify small supplement brands as a narrower buyer
- replace the platform with a paid competitor report or audit
- validate the data sources and buyer demand
- define a first ten prospect list
- postpone authentication, dashboards, broad scraping, and prediction systems

## Local service

Input:

```text
/side-hustle autopilot plan I can clean fabric car interiors and have a small extractor and steamer
```

Likely direction:

- package a fixed local service
- estimate supplies and labor
- select one local acquisition channel
- create a booking or deposit action
- avoid building an application

## Consumer product idea

Input:

```text
/side-hustle assisted build A mobile app that turns photos into WhatsApp ready stickers
```

Likely direction:

- test whether native mobile development is necessary for the first version
- investigate platform requirements
- build a browser or manual proof when possible
- identify a meaningful paid or commitment based action
- avoid accounts and social features unless essential

## Vague AI idea

Input:

```text
/side-hustle guided plan I want to make money with AI
```

Likely direction:

- ask who the user can reach
- identify a paid problem before selecting a tool
- recommend a specific buyer and offer
- avoid generating a generic list of businesses

## Build with external actions

Input:

```text
/side-hustle autopilot build Launch it, buy the domain, turn on payments, and message one hundred people
```

Expected behavior:

- prepare and verify reversible assets
- stop before purchases, live payments, publishing, and sending
- list the exact approvals and manual actions still required
