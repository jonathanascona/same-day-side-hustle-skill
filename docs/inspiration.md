# Inspiration

## The original problem

Many project ideas move directly from excitement to implementation. That often creates polished code before the builder has answered simpler questions:

- Who is the buyer?
- What result matters enough to pay for?
- What can be delivered today?
- How will the first ten people hear about it?
- What action would count as real evidence?

The purpose of this skill is to put those decisions before unnecessary implementation.

## Book and summary

The initial framework was inspired by:

- Chris Guillebeau, *Side Hustle: From Idea to Income in 27 Days*
- Niklas Göke's summary on Four Minute Books

Official and source links:

- https://chrisguillebeau.com/side-hustle-book-idea-to-income/
- https://fourminutebooks.com/side-hustle-summary/

The book presents side hustles as low risk income experiments that can be developed without immediately leaving a primary job.

## The three ideas adapted here

### Feasible, profitable, and persuasive

The skill converts these qualities into a weighted scorecard, hard gates, evidence labels, and a confidence grade.

### Promise, pitch, and price

The skill converts an idea into a buyer centered offer with a clear outcome, delivery explanation, price test, risk reduction, and call to action.

### A minimal launch system

The original summary describes a website, one social profile, scheduling, and payment. This project generalizes those into four functions:

1. Presence
2. Acquisition
3. Fulfillment
4. Conversion

The specific tools can change. The functions remain useful across services, software, reports, workshops, local businesses, and digital products.

## Original additions

This project adds its own operating rules, including:

- hard gates for legality, buyer access, economics, fulfillment, time, and dependencies
- explicit evidence levels
- separate long term vision and same day sellable slice
- business model selection before technical stack selection
- conservative, base, and optimistic scenario modeling
- a launch truth standard
- approval boundaries for spending, publishing, payments, and outreach
- portable build documents for coding agents
- verification and 48 hour decision thresholds
