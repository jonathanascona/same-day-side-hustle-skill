# Opportunity Scoring Model

Score the proposed same day version, not the distant vision.

## Hard gates

A hard gate failure overrides the total. Mark `STOP`, `PIVOT`, or `VALIDATE FIRST`.

1. **Legality and ethics:** The offer appears unlawful, deceptive, harmful, or dependent on violating platform terms, privacy, intellectual property rights, or data rights.
2. **Buyer absence:** No specific buyer can be named and there is no credible way to identify one today.
3. **No conversion path:** There is no realistic paid ask or behavioral commitment.
4. **Negative unit economics:** Expected variable cost is equal to or above price with no strategic reason or correction.
5. **Unfulfillable promise:** The user cannot credibly deliver the result or a safe pilot.
6. **Capital mismatch:** Required upfront cost exceeds the user's stated budget.
7. **Time mismatch:** The proposed version cannot reach buyers today and has no smaller validation artifact.
8. **Dependency risk:** Success requires unavailable credentials, approvals, proprietary data, inventory, or a third party with no fallback.

## Feasible: 30 points

### Same day launchability: 10

- 0 to 2: Cannot be exposed to buyers today.
- 3 to 5: A waitlist or mockup is possible, but the offer is not fulfillable.
- 6 to 8: A credible manual or narrow version can launch.
- 9 to 10: The full buyer to fulfillment path can be tested today.

### Seven day evidence path: 8

Can the user obtain meaningful evidence, such as payment, booking, qualified reply, usage, or rejection, within seven days?

### Operating fit: 6

Can this run alongside the user's main obligations, ideally in about an hour a day after setup?

### Cost and reversibility: 6

Can it start within budget, with low commitments and easy iteration?

## Profitable: 35 points

### Paid problem and buyer: 10

Is the pain or desired outcome important enough that this buyer already spends time, money, or reputation on it?

### Unit economics: 10

Is there believable gross profit after fees, materials, tools, and variable labor?

### Price plausibility: 7

Is the proposed price supported by value, alternatives, anchors, access, or a sensible experiment?

### Path to first transaction: 8

Can the user make a direct, specific offer to reachable buyers now?

## Persuasive: 35 points

### Two sentence clarity: 8

Can a nonexpert understand the buyer, outcome, and deliverable without explanation?

### Strength of promise: 8

Does the offer make a concrete and desirable improvement visible?

### Differentiation or wow: 7

Is there a meaningful reason to choose this offer over doing nothing or using an alternative?

### Reachable audience and channel: 7

Can the first ten prospects be identified and contacted through one channel?

### Founder advantage and trust: 5

Does the user have credible skill, access, speed, insight, proof, or a believable proof building plan?

## Evidence status

Attach one label to every score:

- `proven`: direct behavior or verified operational evidence
- `supported`: credible external evidence or strong analogous proof
- `assumed`: reasonable but untested
- `unknown`: missing and decision relevant

A score built mostly on assumptions must not be described as validated.

## Decision bands

- **80 to 100, Launch:** Build and expose the smallest paid version.
- **65 to 79, Narrow:** Reduce scope or risk, then launch a demand test.
- **50 to 64, Rework:** Fix the weakest major dimension before building.
- **Below 50, Reject or pivot:** Do not spend the day building this version.

## Confidence grade

- **A:** Most material claims are proven or supported.
- **B:** Some material assumptions remain, with quick tests available.
- **C:** The decision rests mainly on assumptions.
- **D:** Critical information is unknown or contradictory.

Do not alter the numeric score to hide uncertainty. Report both.
