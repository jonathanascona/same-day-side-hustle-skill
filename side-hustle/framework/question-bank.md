# Question Bank

Ask only questions whose answers can change the decision, offer, price, scope, channel, or build.

## Core questions

1. Who would feel this problem strongly enough to act this week?
2. What are they trying to avoid, achieve, save, earn, or feel?
3. What can you personally deliver today that moves them toward that outcome?
4. Where could you identify or contact ten plausible buyers today?
5. How many hours and how much cash can you invest before receiving evidence?
6. Why are you unusually able to create or deliver this?
7. What evidence already suggests this problem or offer is real?
8. What would make paying easier than continuing with the current problem?
9. What happens immediately after someone says yes?
10. What observable event would make today a successful launch?

## Guided mode ordering

Do not mechanically ask every question.

- Unclear market: ask about the buyer.
- Interesting solution but vague pain: ask about the outcome.
- Strong pain but huge product: ask about the deliverable.
- Clear offer but no distribution: ask about access.
- Technically ambitious: ask about time and budget.
- Price disagreement: ask about the current alternative and cost of the problem.

## Assisted mode format

Use this structure:

**Recommended assumption:** [specific answer]

**Why:** [one sentence]

**Change it only if:** [condition that would materially change the plan]

Ask the user to accept or replace it only when necessary.

## Questions to avoid

Do not ask broad tone, branding, feature, or stack questions before the offer passes its gates. Do not ask questions already answered in the conversation.

## When the user is unsure

Recommend the option with the fastest access to buyers, easiest manual fulfillment, strongest paid pain, lowest startup cost, and shortest evidence cycle.
