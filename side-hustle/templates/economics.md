## Formulas

- Gross profit before labor equals price minus payment and platform fees minus variable materials and tools.
- Contribution after variable labor equals gross profit before labor minus estimated variable labor value.
- Break even sales equals fixed setup cost divided by contribution per sale.
- Effective hourly gross profit equals gross profit before labor divided by fulfillment hours.
