# Same Day Launch Rules

## Definition

A same day launch is a tested offer placed in front of at least one real acquisition path, with a functioning buyer action and a fulfillment plan.

It is not a finished codebase with no buyer path, a logo and social account, a private mockup, a generic waitlist, a long roadmap, research with no offer, or an untested payment button.

## Default sequence

1. **Decide:** Choose buyer, paid pain, sellable slice, and stop conditions.
2. **Package:** Write promise, pitch, price, deliverables, CTA, and objections.
3. **Create:** Build the smallest presence and fulfillment artifact.
4. **Connect:** Add payment, booking, application, preorder, or tracking.
5. **Verify:** Test the entire path on desktop and mobile where relevant.
6. **Acquire:** Prepare one post and direct outreach to ten likely buyers.
7. **Observe:** Record replies, clicks, calls, deposits, payments, and objections.
8. **Decide again:** Kill, revise, or scale based on evidence.

## Launch checklist

- specific buyer
- specific problem or outcome
- promise
- pitch
- price or explicit pricing test
- one CTA
- public or shareable presence
- one acquisition channel
- fulfillment process
- conversion path
- basic event tracking or manual tracking
- end to end test
- ten prospect definition
- outreach copy
- 48 hour success threshold
- kill or revision threshold

## 48 hour decision examples

- Continue if two of ten qualified prospects request details.
- Continue if one prospect pays a deposit.
- Revise the promise if people reply but misunderstand the offer.
- Revise the channel if the right buyers do not see the message.
- Revise the price if qualified buyers want the result but consistently reject the price.
- Stop the current version if ten relevant conversations reveal the problem is not important.

Do not treat silence from an unqualified audience as proof that the idea is bad.
