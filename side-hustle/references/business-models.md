# Same Day Business Model Selection

Choose the model that can create trustworthy evidence fastest.

## Paid manual service

Use when the user has a useful skill, the outcome can be delivered personally, automation is not proven, and buyers can be reached directly.

Strong evidence includes a paid pilot, deposit, or booked consultation.

## Productized service

Use when the deliverable and scope can be standardized, the buyer wants certainty, and fulfillment can later be automated.

## Audit, report, dataset, template, or digital deliverable

Use when the user can create reusable knowledge or analysis and the value is speed, organization, or insight.

## Workshop or consultation

Use when the buyer needs guidance or a decision and trust matters more than software.

## Concierge MVP

Use when the vision is software, the result can be performed manually behind a simple interface, and the workflow still needs to be learned.

## Narrow software utility

Use when automation itself creates the value, a vertical slice is buildable today, and no complex network, proprietary data, or approval is required.

## Waitlist or free tool

Use only when the paid offer is not credible yet or the market requires trust or critical mass. A generic email signup is weak evidence. Add a qualifying question, use case, budget range, or willingness to pilot.

## Selection rule

When two models can test the same hypothesis, choose the one with faster first transaction, lower setup cost, less technical risk, more direct customer learning, and easier fulfillment.
